Eligibility

Built for creators with original ownership.

You do not need to diagnose your channel before applying. These signals simply help set accurate expectations.

Often a stronger fit

  • You create and publish primarily original videos.
  • You own or control the rights to the submitted content.
  • Your channel has a consistent body of original work.
  • You are not already bound to another rights-management provider for the same content.
  • You can identify your official creator accounts.

May require closer review

  • Reaction, compilation, or commentary formats using substantial third-party footage.
  • Gameplay or music where underlying rights are unclear.
  • Fan pages, repost accounts, or licensed clips without clear documentation.
  • Content already administered through another Content ID or DRM relationship.
  • Individual disputed clips without a broader eligible creator account.

A closer-review signal is not an automatic rejection. Final eligibility depends on the actual accounts and rights involved.

Questions, answered plainly

No black box.

Does Apex provide the protection technology?
No. Apex provides intake, initial eligibility screening, communication, and coordination. Any detection or rights-management technology and related services are provided by independent third parties under their own terms.
Does applying mean my channel is approved?
No. Application begins an eligibility review. Originality, ownership, content type, account history, existing rights relationships, and partner criteria can all affect the decision.
Does this scan the entire internet?
No service should make that promise. Matching and rights-management capabilities depend on participating platforms, the available tools, and the content involved.
Can I submit a video I believe was stolen?
Yes. You can include an optional example link in the application. That helps with context, but submitting a link does not guarantee detection, removal, blocking, or monetization.
What content is most likely to qualify?
Creators with a consistent library of original work and clear ownership are generally the strongest fit. Reaction, compilation, gameplay, music, and licensed-footage formats may require closer review.
Will Apex ask for my password or payment details?
Not in this eligibility form. Do not submit passwords, channel credentials, government identification, banking information, or tax details here.
